## Overview

An agentic alumni platform built for Andhra University's centenary initiative.

## What it does

The platform matches students with alumni, surfaces opportunities, retrieves institutional information, and supports automated follow-up.

## Architecture

RAG + vector retrieval + agentic workflows.

## Recognition

1st place at the AU@100 University Hackathon.

## Technology

LangGraph, RAG, ChromaDB, autonomous agents.
