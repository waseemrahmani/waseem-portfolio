## Overview

AI-powered invoice automation designed to remove repetitive manual data entry from a financial workflow.

## The Problem

Processing purchase and sales invoices manually consumed roughly three hours a day for around 20 invoices.

## Approach

DocuFlow extracts structured invoice data through a provider-agnostic AI layer that can target hosted models or an offline local vision model.

## Workflow

Invoice → Extraction → Validation → Ledger creation → Verification

## Engineering Decisions

The system was designed so extraction does not depend on a single model vendor. Offline inference was optimized for ordinary hardware, and downstream writes were verified with read-back checks.

## Results

Manual data entry was reduced from roughly three hours per day to under twenty minutes.

## Technology

FastAPI, React, Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Ollama.
